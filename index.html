Data Sources

Bike Network: Portland Open Data — Bicycle Network
Race Data: ACS 5-Year Estimates 2024, Table B02001
Income Data: ACS 5-Year Estimates 2024, Table B19013
City Boundaries: Portland Open Data — City Boundaries

Methodology

Bike lane density = total active bike lane miles ÷ tract area (sq mi). Bike segments are clipped to tract boundaries using a spatial overlay in UTM projection; lane length is measured from the clipped geometry to avoid double-counting segments that cross tract lines. All facility types (BL, PBL, BBL, TRL, etc.) weighted equally. Race categories follow OMB single-race-alone definitions; Hispanic is an overlapping ethnicity.

Context

This analysis connects to Portland's Vision Zero goals and the Portland Bureau of Transportation's stated equity commitments. Results should inform how the city prioritizes capital investment in cycling infrastructure.
